VOICE INPUT

Having trouble with voice input?

  • ✓ Allow microphone access in your browser.
  • ✓ Speak clearly and at a normal speed.
  • ✓ Use a quiet environment when possible.
  • ✓ You can always use text input instead.

REPORT UPLOAD

Tips for better document scanning

  • ✓ Keep the document flat and visible.
  • ✓ Use a clear and well-lit image.
  • ✓ Avoid blurry or cropped documents.
  • ✓ Check extracted information before submission.
